Speed and Number Plates

One camera stream finds each vehicle, keeps its identity, estimates speed from how it moves, and reads the plate when the crop is clear.

YOLOSORTTrOCR
  1. 01 · FindYOLOv8n, trained on COCO, boxes the vehicles in the frame.
  2. 02 · TrackSORT gives each box an id, so the same car stays the same car.
  3. 03 · MeasureSpeed is how far that id moves between frames, scaled into km/h.
  4. 04 · ReadA second YOLO finds the plate inside the vehicle. TrOCR reads that crop.

Question

  • Can a single video say which vehicle this is, how fast it is going, and what the plate says?
  • Plates are small, and the same car must not become a new id every frame.

Why this path

  • Detection without a tracker cannot measure speed. The id is what makes the motion belong to one car.
  • The plate model is separate because a plate is a thin object inside a much larger box.

What held

~0.95plate detector, mAP50
1 idper vehicle, held by SORT
1 streamannotated video in the browser

Reading the result

  • On the sample highway clip the tracker draws a box, an id, and a speed. Two of those tracks read about 41 km/h and 37 km/h.
  • When the vehicle is large in the frame, the plate box appears and OCR returns characters from that crop.
  • The same pipeline was packed in Docker and pointed at a live camera on Azure Container Instances.

A real output

sample clip, one frame ID 3 41 km/h ID 5 37 km/h plate read when the crop is large enough

Open the video feed to see the boxes move.

Where the energy went

  • Detection and tracking are the solid half. They are what the stream shows immediately.
  • Reading every character, and smoothing the speed so it does not jump between frames, is the pass that still tightens.

Next pass. Plate text is reliable on a close crop and thinner on a distant one. Speed is a frame-to-frame estimate and wants a short average before anyone treats it as a measurement.

Stack

YOLOv8n, SORT, TrOCR, FastAPI. Weights load from plate_detection_env.
